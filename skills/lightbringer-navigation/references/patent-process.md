# Patent process orientation

Use this overview to explain the patent journey and locate a relevant next decision. It uses a Swedish first-application route as its baseline, with separate international branches. Many broad stages recur elsewhere, but routes, procedures, deadlines and outcomes vary. Do not infer the user's filing office from the organisation's home country or require a Swedish first filing.

## Customer-facing stages

The Lightbringer column describes the purpose of professional work within the agreed engagement, not a claim that it has started, is included in a particular plan or can be executed through MCP. Keep internal steps general. Explain the stages relevant to the question rather than reciting a fixed sequence.

| Stage | Lightbringer's role | Customer contribution and useful result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capture the invention and choose a direction | Help clarify the invention and its business context. | Supply supported technical facts and goals; obtain a usable description and open questions. Saving the description and requesting preparation are separate actions. |
| Request preparation and hear from the team | The request notifies the Lightbringer team, who will get back to the user before further work proceeds. | Identify the invention and express the request; receive its confirmed request status. A paid plan, assigned specialist or attorney, and the start of professional work must not be assumed. |
| Assess and prepare the application | Assess the invention and relevant prior art, develop the drafting approach and prepare application material. | Answer material questions and provide technical corrections; receive the assessment or draft when available. Detailed internal task order is outside navigation. |
| Review and finalise | Work through feedback and prepare the filing package. | Review the draft, confirm applicant/inventor details and give required filing instructions or approvals; work toward an agreed filing version. |
| File and confirm | Coordinate submission and check the official filing evidence. | Complete any outstanding requested decisions; receive confirmed filing details when available. Approval of a draft alone does not establish submission. |
| Examination and responses | Explain the patent office's findings and prepare responses within the professional engagement. | Supply technical input and decisions when requested; follow the actual notices, responses and confirmed case status. Further response cycles may follow. |
| Publication | Help explain and track the application's public availability where covered by the service. | Understand what has become public and the implications to discuss with the team. Publication is a separate event from grant and can overlap examination. |
| Grant and continued protection | Coordinate applicable grant formalities and explain maintenance or further decisions. | Confirm relevant choices and obtain the official outcome. Renewal arrangements, opposition, refusal, appeal or stopping require attention when applicable; none should be inferred from a typical timeline. |

The Swedish stage framework is described in [PRV's advanced patent guide](https://www.prv.se/en/patents/the-advanced-patent-guide/). [PRV's examination guidance](https://www.prv.se/en/patents/the-advanced-patent-guide/examination-of-the-application/) distinguishes formal and technical examination; its [grant guidance](https://www.prv.se/en/patents/the-advanced-patent-guide/patent-granted/) distinguishes pre-grant steps from the official grant. These authorities describe patent procedures, not Lightbringer service availability.

## International and conditional routes

Territory decisions depend on the user's goals and the actual application. Direct foreign filings and the PCT route are alternatives to discuss, not compulsory stages for every Swedish application. Under the PCT, an international application and its search/opinion can precede selected national or regional phases; the PCT does not grant a worldwide patent. Each destination has its own procedure and outcome. See [WIPO's PCT overview and questions](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/pct-system/faqs/faqs).

European grant can lead to national validation and/or a request for unitary effect where applicable; these are European-route decisions, not automatic steps following a Swedish national grant. Use the [EPO's unitary-patent guidance](https://www.epo.org/en/applying/european/unitary) and the relevant office's current rules for the selected route. Treat a US provisional application as a separate route needing its own guidance, not a renamed Swedish application.

## Timing, status and handoffs

Reference timelines illustrate stages and possible overlaps; they are not customer deadlines or promises of grant. Establish the actual jurisdiction, route, official event and relevant date before discussing case-specific timing. Distinguish elapsed-time estimates from legal deadlines, and priority-based, filing-based and grant-based dates. Verify current official guidance and the case's notices when answering a deadline question; do not calculate one from an illustrative month label. If the necessary evidence is missing, retain the uncertainty and direct the user to the patent team for confirmation.

The process references above were consulted on 28 October 2026. Recheck applicable official guidance for jurisdiction-specific requirements and time-sensitive answers. A process explanation or automated observation does not determine this invention's patentability or freedom to operate.
